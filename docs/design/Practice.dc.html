Question 3 of 8 · Choose the right form
Yesterday I ____ the email at 6 p.m.
Almost. Use "finished" here.
With a time that is over, like yesterday, last week or in 2023, English uses the past simple: "I sent", "I finished".
In French, "Hier, j'ai fini" looks like "I have finished", but English does not use "have" with "yesterday".
