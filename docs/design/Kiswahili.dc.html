Salamu: greetings and people

Habari yako?
How are you?
Nzuri, asante.
Fine, thank you.
Jina lako ni nani?
What is your name?
Jina langu ni…
My name is…
Unatoka wapi?
Where are you from?
Ninatoka Rwanda.
I'm from Rwanda.
