You said sorry, gave the reason and a new time. Two small fixes:
You said: "I will be late of 20 minutes."
Better: "I'll be 20 minutes late."
French says "en retard de 20 minutes". English puts the time before "late".
You said: "I will call you when I will arrive."
Better: "I'll call you when I arrive."
After "when" or "as soon as", English uses the present to talk about the future.
